    Choosing the right one

    Razai, dohar or comforter?

    Razai
    A hand-quilted Jaipuri quilt filled with pure cotton. The warmest option, for cold nights or strong AC.
    Dohar
    Three thin layers: printed cotton on both sides with a light falalen sheet inside. Good all year in Chennai, especially under the fan or AC.
    Comforter
    Quilted and washable. In between a razai and a dohar.

    Fabrics

    Mulmul (voile)
    Very fine, soft cotton that breathes well. The coolest to sleep under in summer.
    Cotton filling
    Natural and warm. Air the razai in the sun now and then to keep it fluffy.
    Falalen
    A light synthetic layer inside dohars. Keeps them light and machine washable.

    Sizes

    Single
    About 60 × 90 in (5 × 7.5 ft). For a single or diwan bed.
    Double / King
    90 × 100 to 100 × 108 in (7.5 × 8 to 8 × 9 ft). For a queen or king bed.
    Jumbo king
    108 to 110 in square. Hangs well over the sides of a large king bed.
